Dotnull Inc. (the “Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) establishes this Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) regarding the handling of users’ personal information in the application “MorseLink” (the “App”) provided by the Company.

Article 1 (Information We Collect)

To provide the App, we may collect the following information.

  1. Information you provide
    • Account information: Email address, or authentication information for Google/Apple accounts (OAuth ID)
    • Profile information: Nickname, icon image, and other information you optionally set
  2. Information collected automatically through your use of the App
    • Learning metrics: Energy consumption/recovery history, AI chat history, drill results, quest progress, etc.
    • Device information: Device type (iOS/Android), OS version, language settings, advertising ID (Advertising ID)
    • Log information: Operation logs, crash reports, and performance data collected via Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics
    • Notification token: A token used to send push notifications
    • Cookies and SDKs: The App uses third-party SDKs such as Firebase SDK. These modules may collect and use cookies and advertising IDs to analyze usage and deliver advertisements.

Article 2 (Purposes of Use)

We use the collected information for the following purposes.

  1. To provide, operate, maintain, protect, improve, and enhance the App
  2. To store your learning history, manage progress, and customize your learning experience
  3. To generate responses for the AI coach feature (including sending data to the OpenAI API)
  4. To provide energy management, quest progression, and other gamification features
  5. To respond to inquiries, consultations, and complaints, and to resolve disputes
  6. To inform you about new features, updates, campaigns, etc.
  7. To address violations of the Terms of Service
  8. To create and analyze statistical data in a manner that does not identify individuals, and to conduct marketing activities

Article 3 (Provision to Third Parties / Outsourcing)

We do not provide your personal information to any third party except in the following cases.

  1. With your consent
  2. When required by laws and regulations
  3. When necessary to protect a person’s life, body, or property and it is difficult to obtain consent
  4. When particularly necessary to improve public health or promote the sound growth of children and it is difficult to obtain consent
  5. When it is necessary to cooperate with a national agency, local government, or an entity entrusted by them in performing duties prescribed by laws and regulations, and obtaining your consent may impede the performance of such duties

To the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of use, we may outsource all or part of the handling of personal information to the following third parties. In such cases, we will provide necessary and appropriate supervision to ensure safe management of information by the outsourcing parties.

  • OpenAI, L.L.C.
    • Purpose: Generating responses for the AI chat feature
    • Information provided: Inputs to the AI chat (we may process inputs to exclude information that can identify individuals)
    • Location: United States
  • Google LLC (Firebase)
    • Purpose: User authentication, database, storage, analytics, crash reporting, push notifications
    • Information provided: Account information, learning metrics, device information, log information
    • Location: United States

Article 3-2 (Advertising and Your Privacy Choices)

The free version uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, for banner, full-screen, and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and the advertising partners involved in serving an ad may receive your IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, advertising identifiers where permitted, ad requests, impressions and interactions, and your privacy choices. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, personalize ads where permitted, research audiences, develop and improve advertising services, and detect fraud and abuse. Advertising SDKs may use cookies or other storage on your device.

Google's use of information is explained in its Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where a consent message is shown, its advertising-partner list provides the applicable partners and links to their policies. The Company does not pass your learning answers, practice text, or chat messages to AdMob as advertising-targeting parameters.

Where consent is required, including in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the updated app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present the applicable choices before requesting ads. You may decline consent; the app does not require consent to personalized advertising to continue learning. Depending on your choices and availability, you may receive limited ads or no ads. Limited ads do not use personal data for ad personalization, but may still process an IP address to deliver an ad and use cookies or local storage solely to detect fraud and abuse.

In versions with this feature, you can review or change your choice in Settings > Advertising privacy settings whenever the applicable consent platform requires this option. If the settings cannot be loaded, you can retry there. Changing a choice applies to subsequent ad requests; it does not erase earlier processing. Older versions may not have this control; update the app or contact the address in Article 9. Your device's advertising or tracking settings provide additional controls. On iOS, permission for tracking under App Tracking Transparency is separate from advertising consent; a consent choice does not grant that system permission.

Ad-related information held by Google or its partners is handled under their own retention and deletion policies. Deleting your MorseLink account does not automatically delete information those providers retain independently. Use their privacy controls or contact us for help with an applicable access, deletion, objection, or withdrawal request.

Article 4 (International Data Transfers)

As described in Article 3, we provide your information to third parties located in the United States. For information regarding personal information protection systems in the United States, please refer to information provided by Japan’s Personal Information Protection Commission.

Reference: Survey of systems related to the protection of personal information in foreign countries

Article 5 (Retention Period and Requests for Deletion)

  1. We retain your information while you have an account for the App.
  2. If you withdraw from the App, we will delete your information within 30 days after withdrawal unless retention is required by laws and regulations or business necessity.
  3. You may request disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, or suspension of use of your personal information. If you wish to make such a request, please contact us at the contact point in Article 9 with information that allows us to verify your identity (e.g., your email address). After confirming your identity, we will respond promptly in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Article 6 (Security Measures)

We strive to keep your personal information accurate and up-to-date and take the following security measures to protect it from unauthorized access, tampering, leakage, loss, or damage.

  • Encrypted communications: Communications between your device and our servers are encrypted using SSL/TLS.
  • Access controls: Access to personal information is limited to employees who need it for business purposes, and we implement access controls.
  • Incident response: In the event of a leakage incident or similar, we will report to relevant authorities and notify users promptly in accordance with laws and regulations.

Article 7 (Push Notifications)

To support your continued learning, we may send push notifications such as cooldown completion notices, summaries of unread messages, and quest suggestions. You can stop these notifications at any time from your device settings.

Article 8 (Changes to This Privacy Policy)

We may revise this Policy as necessary. If we make changes to this Policy that require user consent under laws and regulations, the revised Policy will apply only to users who have consented to the changes by a method prescribed by the Company. When we revise this Policy, we will notify users of the effective date and contents of the revised Policy by displaying it on our website or by other appropriate methods, or by directly notifying users.

Article 9 (Contact)

For opinions, questions, complaints, and other inquiries regarding the handling of user information, please contact:

DOTNULL Co.,Ltd
support@morse-link.com

第3条の2（広告配信とプライバシーの選択）

無料版では、Google LLC が提供する Google AdMob を利用して、バナー広告、全画面広告、および任意で視聴するリワード広告を配信します。Google および広告配信に関与する広告パートナーは、IPアドレス、IPアドレスから推定されるおおまかな地域、端末・アプリ情報、利用が認められる場合の広告識別子、広告リクエスト・表示・操作の情報、およびプライバシーの選択内容を受け取ることがあります。これらは広告の配信・効果測定、認められる場合の広告のパーソナライズ、広告に関する調査、広告サービスの開発・改善、不正や不正利用の検知・防止に利用されます。広告SDKは、端末上のCookieやその他のストレージを利用する場合があります。

Google による情報の取扱いは、Google プライバシーポリシーおよびGoogle のサービスを使用するアプリから収集した情報の使用で確認できます。同意メッセージが表示される場合は、その広告パートナー一覧で対象事業者と各事業者のポリシーも確認できます。当社は、学習の回答、練習用テキスト、チャット本文を広告ターゲティングのパラメーターとして AdMob に渡しません。

EEA（欧州経済領域）・英国・スイスを含む同意が必要な地域では、対応版のアプリが Google の User Messaging Platform（UMP）を使用し、広告をリクエストする前に対象となる選択肢を提示します。同意しない選択も可能であり、パーソナライズ広告への同意を学習継続の条件にはしません。選択内容や広告の提供状況により、制限付きの広告が表示されるか、広告が表示されない場合があります。制限付きの広告では個人データを広告のパーソナライズに使用しませんが、広告を届けるためのIPアドレスの処理や、不正検知・防止専用のCookie・ローカルストレージの利用が行われる場合があります。

本機能の対応版では、対象となる同意管理プラットフォームが変更導線を必要とする場合、「設定」内の「広告のプライバシー設定」から選択内容を確認・変更できます。設定を取得できない場合は、同じ項目から再試行できます。選択の変更は以後の広告リクエストに適用され、過去の処理を取り消すものではありません。旧版に本機能がない場合は、アプリを更新するか第9条の窓口へお問い合わせください。端末の広告・トラッキング設定からも制御できます。iOS の App Tracking Transparency によるトラッキング許可は広告の同意とは別のもので、広告の同意によってOSの許可が付与されることはありません。

Google や広告パートナーが保有する広告関連情報には、各事業者の保存・削除方針が適用されます。MorseLink のアカウントを削除しても、各事業者が独立して保持する情報が自動的に削除されるものではありません。適用される開示・削除・異議申立て・同意撤回のご請求は、各事業者のプライバシー管理機能をご利用いただくか、当社へお問い合わせください。

第6条（安全管理措置）

当社は、お客様の個人情報を正確かつ最新の内容に保つよう努め、不正なアクセス・改ざん・漏えい・滅失及び毀損から保護するため、以下の安全管理措置を講じます。

  • 通信の暗号化: お客様の端末と当社サーバー間の通信は、SSL/TLSにより暗号化しています。
  • アクセス制限: 個人情報へのアクセスは、業務上必要な従業員に限定し、アクセス制御を実施しています。
  • インシデント対応: 個人情報の漏えい等の事故が発生した場合、法令に従い、速やかに関係機関への報告およびお客様への通知を行います。
